Color System

Three colors anchor everything: the emblem blue, the emblem red, and white. Every additional color is a tint or shade of those — or a calm neutral that gets out of their way.

Color usage rules

The 60·30·10 rule60% neutral surfaces (white, paper, ink-75) · 30% USMLE Blue for structure and trust · 10% Wise Red exclusively for CTAs and energy moments.
Red is preciousNever use Wise Red as a large background, body text color, or for routine UI chrome. Reserve it for primary CTAs, key statistics, and one accent moment per screen.

Density rules

Inline (4–8 px)
Between an icon and its label, between badges in a row, between a value and its unit.
Component (12–24 px)
Card padding, button padding, gaps between siblings in a flex row.
Block (32–48 px)
Vertical rhythm between content blocks, hero internal padding, dashboard widget gaps.
Section (64–96 px)
Page-level breathers. Always 96 px between top-level marketing sections.
