ECFMG certification is not one application. It is several requirements, each with its own queue, and some can only begin after others finish. The shortest path is to start the slowest external items first. This page gives the sequence, what depends on what, and where time usually leaks. For what each requirement means, see the [certification overview](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-certification-overview).

## The sequence

| Order | Step | Depends on | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check school and graduation-year eligibility in the [World Directory](/wiki/ecfmg/world-directory-and-school-eligibility) | Nothing | An ineligible school or year cannot be fixed by exams. See also the [accreditation requirement](/wiki/ecfmg/accreditation-requirement). |
| 2 | Create your [MyIntealth account](/wiki/ecfmg/myintealth-account) | Name matching your passport | Identity problems follow you through every later step. |
| 3 | Apply for ECFMG certification | Account | Opens your file and starts the credential steps. |
| 4 | Credential verification ([EPIC](/wiki/ecfmg/epic-credential-verification)) | Application, school cooperation | Usually the slowest step because your school must reply. |
| 5 | USMLE Step 1 and Step 2 CK | Eligibility from ECFMG; registration through FSMB since January 2026 | See [eligibility](/wiki/exams/usmle-eligibility-for-imgs) and [when to take each Step](/wiki/exams/when-to-take-each-step). |
| 6 | Clinical and communication skills through a Pathway, including OET Medicine | Pathway eligibility and an OET result | See the [OET guide](/wiki/ecfmg/occupational-english-test). |
| 7 | ERAS token request | Conditions ECFMG sets for tokens | See the [ERAS token guide](/wiki/ecfmg/eras-token). |
| 8 | Certificate issued | All requirements met and accepted | Programs and the NRMP set their own cutoffs for proof. |

> [!NOTE] Since January 2026, IMGs register for USMLE Steps through FSMB, while ECFMG still decides eligibility for certification. If an older guide tells you to register for Steps in MyIntealth, it is out of date.

## What can run in parallel

- **Credential verification and exam study.** Start verification early so it is moving while you study.
- **OET and exams.** The OET is independent of the Steps, but your Pathway application has its own eligibility rules. Check them first.
- **ERAS preparation.** You can draft your application while final requirements finish. See the [ERAS timeline](/wiki/application/eras-timeline).

## Work backward from the Match

1. Pick your Match year and find the NRMP's certification and eligibility deadlines on its site. They are set annually.
2. Identify the external step that will finish last. For many IMGs that is the Pathway result or Step 2 CK.
3. Add buffer for a retake or a slow response. This is planning prudence, not an official figure.
4. Start credential verification before everything else, because you do not control your school's pace.

The [rank order list guide](/wiki/interviews-match/rank-order-list-strategy) explains what happens when the deadline arrives and your status is checked.

## Realistic delay points

| Delay point | Typical cause | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Credential verification | School is slow, wrong contact, documents need translation | Contact the school early and keep proof of what you sent |
| Name and ID mismatches | Names differ between passport, diploma and exam registration | Resolve before any application |
| Exam scheduling | Test-center availability, visa or travel issues | See [Prometric scheduling](/wiki/exams/scheduling-with-prometric) |
| OET results | Seat availability, retakes, results not released to ECFMG | Book early and verify receipt |
| Pathway processing | Application windows are seasonal and rules change by year | Check ECFMG's current Pathways page |

More detail is in [common ECFMG delays](/wiki/ecfmg/common-ecfmg-delays), and the money side is in [ECFMG fees](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-fees).

## What is different for IMGs

All of this is IMG-specific. Three points trip people up: your **graduation year** determines whether your school's listing covers you, the rules are reissued every year in the ECFMG Information Booklet, and some options (Pathway application windows) open and close on set dates. For the big picture across exams, ECFMG, ERAS and visas, see [how long does it take](/wiki/start-here/how-long-does-it-take).

> [!WARNING] Do not trust a single timeline from social media. Dates and windows are published by ECFMG, the USMLE program and the NRMP, and they move.
