Your MyIntealth account is the front door to everything ECFMG does for you: certification, document uploads, exam-related services and your ERAS token. You create it online at the MyIntealth Applicant Portal, then prove your identity with a notarized form. Doing this carefully the first time saves weeks later.

## Before you start

Gather these items first. ECFMG asks for:

- your name exactly as it appears on a **current, unexpired passport**
- your date of birth and address of residence
- your medical school information
- a full-color photograph of your face
- a scanned, full-color copy of the photo page of your passport

File types and size limits for the photo and passport scan are set by ECFMG and have been described differently in different documents, so check the limits shown during the application itself.

> [!WARNING] If the information you enter does not match your passport, ECFMG will change the name, gender and date of birth in your record to match the passport. Do not guess or use a shortened name. Match the passport exactly.

## Step by step

1. **Open the MyIntealth Applicant Portal** and enter your email address to receive a verification code.
2. **Use the email address ECFMG already has on record** if you used ECFMG's former online services (OASIS). That links your existing account to MyIntealth instead of starting a new one.
3. **Answer the prior-application question honestly.** If you have ever applied to ECFMG, even under another name, say yes and enter your USMLE ID if you know it.
4. **Submit your account request** with your details, photo and passport scan.
5. **Wait for processing.** After your request is processed, ECFMG creates a personalized Intealth Identification Form (IIF).
6. **Complete the IIF and have it notarized.** This is normally done through NotaryCam, a live online session with a licensed US notary. You need the same identification you uploaded.
7. **Release the notarized form** so ECFMG can complete identity verification.

You have six months from the date your personalized IIF is created to finish notarization, so do not leave it sitting.

## Your IDs

| ID | What it is |
|---|---|
| MyIntealth ID | Issued when you establish your account. You get one, it cannot be changed, and you can find it by logging in. ECFMG will not give it out by phone. |
| USMLE ID | Since the USMLE service transition in January 2026, FSMB issues the USMLE ID to IMGs when they register for a first Step exam. IDs previously issued by ECFMG remain valid. |

Keep a record of both, and keep your login email secure.

## What is different for IMGs

US medical graduates do not use this process. For IMGs, identity verification is a gate: you generally cannot move forward on credentials, certification or an ERAS token until the account is established and identity is verified. Starting early is the simplest way to protect your timeline. The [ERAS token guide](/wiki/ecfmg/eras-token) explains why.

## Common mistakes

- **Name mismatch.** The most common cause of delays. Your name should read the same on your passport, medical diploma references and exam registrations. If your diploma name differs, plan for extra documentation and read the ECFMG guidance before you submit.
- **Expired or soon-to-expire passport.** Renew before you apply.
- **Creating a second account** because you forgot the first.
- **Ignoring emails.** ECFMG communicates through MyIntealth and email. Check spam folders.
- **Using an unofficial site.** Only use the portal linked from ecfmg.org. ECFMG does not endorse paid services that offer to set up your account for you.

> [!NOTE] Portal screens and file limits change. Treat this page as orientation and follow the instructions inside MyIntealth and the official user guide as the authority.
