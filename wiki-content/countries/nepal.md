If you graduated from a medical school in Nepal, start with one question: does your school's ECFMG Sponsor Note cover your graduation year? Everything else, from exams to visas, depends on the answer. This guide covers what is known about Nepali IMGs in published data, how to check eligibility, testing, documents and the US visa process. It is general information, not legal advice.

## What the published data say about IMGs from Nepal

NRMP reports IMGs by citizenship, not by country of medical school. In the 2026 Main Residency Match, 11,944 non-U.S. citizen IMGs submitted rank order lists and 6,733 matched to a PGY-1 position, a match rate of 56.4 percent. NRMP's July 2026 Charting Outcomes report for non-U.S. IMGs breaks results down by preferred specialty, not by country.

We found no Nepal-specific match number in the NRMP reports we downloaded. ECFMG's certification fact sheet reports totals only. We do not quote a Nepal figure, and we suggest you distrust any that lacks a named source and year. See [IMG match statistics](/wiki/start-here/img-match-statistics) for how to read what is published.

## Checking eligibility

ECFMG requires a listing in the World Directory of Medical Schools with an ECFMG Sponsor Note covering your graduation year, and since 2024 it has required accreditation by an agency recognized by the World Federation for Medical Education (WFME). ECFMG states that the WFME Recognition Programme is the only program it accepts for recognizing accrediting agencies. Follow the steps in the [World Directory guide](/wiki/ecfmg/world-directory-and-school-eligibility) and [accreditation requirement](/wiki/ecfmg/accreditation-requirement).

What we could verify about Nepal:

- **The regulator:** the Nepal Medical Council registers doctors. Registration is not the same as WFME-recognized accreditation of your school.
- **The accreditor:** Nepal's Medical Education Commission (MEC) appears on WFME's list as an agency applying for recognition. A Nepali news site reported that the MEC started the formal process in October 2023. We did not find it among recognized agencies in November 2026.
- **What it means:** we could not open ECFMG's own statement on Nepal, so do not guess from news or from a school's marketing. Your school's listing and the years it states are what ECFMG applies.

> [!WARNING] If your school has no ECFMG Sponsor Note, or your graduation year is not covered, you are not eligible for ECFMG Certification on that basis. Ask ECFMG directly about your options. Do not pay anyone who claims to have a workaround.

If you are still a student, check before final year and ask your school what its accreditation timeline is.

## USMLE testing from Nepal

IMGs register through FSMB since January 2026, with ECFMG deciding certification eligibility. Step 1 and Step 2 CK are given at Prometric centers worldwide; Step 3 only in the US and its territories (USMLE Bulletin). A Kathmandu center is described by third-party sources, but we could not confirm it on usmle.org or Prometric's own pages. Use the Prometric site after you receive your scheduling permit, and prepare a backup location and travel plan. See [USMLE test centers outside the US](/wiki/exams/international-test-centers) and [scheduling with Prometric](/wiki/exams/scheduling-with-prometric).

Score validity is another point. The USMLE can invalidate a score when the pattern of performance is highly irregular and can require a validation exam. The Kathmandu Post reported in February 2024 that some Nepali examinees received such notices; the Nepal Medical Council said the decision belonged to USMLE. We cite that as a news report, not an official USMLE statement. The lesson for everyone: use legitimate preparation materials, and read the Bulletin's rules on irregular behavior. See [exam day rules](/wiki/exams/exam-day-rules-and-checklist).

## Credentials and documents

ECFMG verifies your diploma and transcript directly with the issuing school. Ask your registrar early who handles verification and how long it takes. ECFMG's Reference Guide for Medical Education Credentials gives the diploma title required for your school and year. See [EPIC credential verification](/wiki/ecfmg/epic-credential-verification) and [common ECFMG delays](/wiki/ecfmg/common-ecfmg-delays).

The Kathmandu Post reported that the Nepal Medical Council issues Letters of Good Standing, many of them for doctors headed to the US. Whether ECFMG, a state board or a program requires one is for that body to say, so ask before you request it.

## The US visa process for Nepali nationals

Matched IMGs from Nepal usually need a J-1 (sponsored by ECFMG) or an H-1B. See [J-1 visa process](/wiki/visas/j1-visa-process), [J-1 vs H-1B](/wiki/visas/j1-vs-h1b) and [visa interview preparation](/wiki/visas/visa-interview-preparation). Also read the [two-year home residency rule](/wiki/visas/two-year-home-residency-rule), since it may apply to J-1 physicians.

> [!CHANGED] November 2026: Proclamation 10998, effective January 1, 2026, fully or partly suspends entry for nationals of listed countries. We read the text on whitehouse.gov and Nepal is not named in it. We found no later proclamation adding it. Check the State Department and our [visa policy tracker](/wiki/visas/visa-policy-changes-tracker) before you commit to travel dates.

Interview availability at the US Embassy in Kathmandu changes, and we could not verify current wait times; check its appointment page.
