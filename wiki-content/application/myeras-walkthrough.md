To do the ERAS application, you register in MyERAS (IMGs need an ERAS token from ECFMG first), complete the Application sections, build your Documents, preview everything, then certify and submit and apply to programs. The application has two halves: an **Application** you fill in (personal details, education, experiences, scholarly work, specialty questions) and a **Documents** area where you manage your personal statement, letters of recommendation, USMLE transcript, MSPE and medical school transcript. This guide follows the order of AAMC's 2027 MyERAS Applicant User Guide, so you can open it beside this page.

> [!NOTE] Section names, limits and options are set by AAMC and change between cycles. Treat the numbers below as the 2027 cycle's, and check the current user guide if you are reading this for a later season. For the calendar, see the [ERAS timeline](/wiki/application/eras-timeline).

> For the 2027 Match, NRMP says Emergency Medicine and Obstetrics and Gynecology programs use ResidencyCAS rather than ERAS, and all other specialties use ERAS. If you are applying to either of those two specialties, the ERAS steps, fees and signaling described on this page may not apply to them. Read the [Emergency Medicine](/wiki/specialties/emergency-medicine) and [OB-GYN](/wiki/specialties/obstetrics-and-gynecology) guides, and confirm the current system with NRMP.

## How to do the ERAS application, step by step

1. **Get the token and register.** See below. Delays here push back everything else.
2. **Fill the factual sections first** (personal information, education, exams, dates) from your transcript and certificates.
3. **Draft the experiences and personal statement in a plain text editor,** then paste them in. MyERAS has no spell or grammar check.
4. **Create letter entries early** so authors have time to upload.
5. **Preview the whole application,** then have someone who has read many applications check it.
6. **Save programs and assign signals and documents.** See [program signaling](/wiki/application/program-signaling-explained).
7. **Certify and submit the application.** This is one-time and locks the Application.
8. **Apply to programs, pay, and track statuses** for every document.

> [!IMG TIP] Keep one master document with every date, title, supervisor and institution in it. Almost every MyERAS section asks for the same facts in a slightly different shape, and inconsistent dates look careless.

## Before you start: token and registration

IMGs cannot register in MyERAS until ECFMG / Intealth issues an [[eras-token]]. The token is a one-time code tied to your ECFMG record. Read [the ERAS token guide](/wiki/ecfmg/eras-token) first. Once registered, the Dashboard shows your document statuses, USMLE transcript request status and the programs you have saved or applied to.

## The Application tabs

### Personal Information

Your AAMC account details, basic information, address, work authorization and Match information. Work authorization is where many IMGs hesitate. Answer truthfully and consistently with your ECFMG record; programs use this to judge whether they can sponsor you, so see [J-1 vs H-1B](/wiki/visas/j1-vs-h1b) before you answer visa questions.

The Match information selections are where you say whether you intend to take part in the Match individually or as a couple. See the [Couples Match guide](/wiki/interviews-match/couples-match).

### Biographical Information

Self-identification, language fluency, military information, **geographic preferences**, **setting preference** and hometowns.

- **Geographic preferences:** you can pick up to three US Census divisions, or say you have no preference, and explain in text. If you pick a division, only programs in that division see the answer. If you say you have no preference, all your programs see that. If you skip it, no program sees anything.
- **Setting preference:** urban, suburban or rural, or no preference. In the 2027 season some specialties (Otolaryngology, Orthopedic Surgery, Plastic Surgery - Integrated) opted out of receiving these answers.
- Programs can filter applicants by these answers, so be honest rather than strategic. See [how programs screen](/wiki/application/program-screening-filters) and [how to explain a geographic preference](/wiki/application/program-signaling-explained).

### Education, honors and awards, and the academic challenges questions

Higher education, medical education, postgraduate training, interruptions or extensions to training, honors and awards, and professional memberships. Dates must match your transcript and MSPE exactly; mismatches are a common reason for questions at interview.

**The academic challenges question (Interruptions or Extensions).** This is the part of MyERAS applicants search for as the "academic challenges" question. It asks two Yes or No questions, and a Yes needs a description:

1. Have you had any academic extensions, leaves, gaps or breaks in your educational program because of repeated or remediated coursework?
2. Have you had any professional extensions, leaves, gaps or breaks because of professionalism sanctions or other adverse actions by your medical school or its parent institutions?

AAMC says the section is not meant to collect health, disability or family information. Answer truthfully, briefly and consistently with your MSPE and transcript, because programs can compare them. For how to handle related topics, see [gap years](/wiki/application/gap-years-and-how-to-explain) and [failed exam attempts](/wiki/application/failed-attempts-on-application).

**Honors and awards.** In the 2027 guide this entry asks about your current status in Alpha Omega Alpha (MD applicants only), the Gold Humanism Honor Society and, for DO applicants only, Sigma Sigma Phi. If your school has no such chapter, there may be nothing to enter. The guide does not describe a separate field for other prizes or scholarships. As practice (not an AAMC rule), many applicants mention a significant award in the description of the related experience, and keep every honor consistent with the MSPE and CV.

### Experience, including research experiences

Up to {{fact:eras_experiences_max}} selected experiences, up to {{fact:eras_most_meaningful_max}} of which can be marked most meaningful, plus an optional Impactful Experiences question and a short Hobbies and Interests field. This is the section applicants most underuse. It has its own page: [writing the Experiences section](/wiki/application/eras-experiences-section).

**Research experiences** go in two places. In Experience, choose the type Research and describe your role and context. In Scholarly Work (below), enter the outputs. See [how programs view research](/wiki/research/how-programs-view-research) and [research on your application](/wiki/application/research-on-your-application).

### Licenses and Certifications (including BLS)

State medical licenses (or "None"), answers to questions about license status, malpractice and convictions, board certification, other certifications and DEA registration. Most IMG applicants answer None or no to many of these. Answer every question; a blank can block certification.

**BLS, ACLS and other certifications.** The Other Certifications entry asks whether you hold other medical or health care related certifications and lets you enter as many as you need. The 2027 guide does not name BLS or ACLS, but both are health care related certifications, so this is the place built for them. If you are unsure, check the current guide or ask AAMC support. Board certification means specialty board certification, which most applicants do not yet have. Your ECFMG certification reaches programs through the ECFMG Status Report that ECFMG uploads, as described on the [MSPE and documents page](/wiki/application/mspe-and-transcripts-for-imgs).

### Scholarly Work

Journal articles, book chapters and abstracts (peer reviewed), oral presentations and poster presentations. You can group related work into collections and star up to three items as most meaningful. Enter authors in the format the guide specifies and bold your own name. Use the "preview as program" button to check how it appears. Your Designated Dean's Office can advise on labeling. For IMGs with research, see the [research guidance](/wiki/research/how-programs-view-research).

### Specialty Questions

In the 2027 guide, applicants to Anesthesiology, Neurological Surgery and Plastic Surgery - Integrated must answer specialty-specific questions before sending applications to programs in those specialties. You must answer every question for a specialty before you can save, and you cannot edit or delete responses after you certify and submit, though you can still add responses for another specialty later. Read the questions carefully and draft them in plain text first.

### Certify and Submit

Certification is your attestation that the information is accurate. Misstatements can have serious consequences later, including at licensing. The guide says certifying and submitting is a one-time action that cannot be reversed, and you cannot apply to programs until you have done it. Submit only after previewing every section.

## Is there a supplemental ERAS application?

Not as a separate form. In earlier cycles AAMC piloted an optional supplemental ERAS application (its own evaluation report describes the 2022-2023 cycle as the second year of piloting). The 2027 applicant guide describes no separate supplemental application: the Experiences section, Impactful Experiences question, program signals and geographic and setting preferences are sections inside MyERAS. Some websites and advisors still use "supplemental application" as shorthand for those parts. Specialty-specific questions are the one extra step for a few specialties, as described above. Check the current cycle's ERAS instructions, because AAMC continues to evaluate pilot projects.

## The Documents tab

| Document | What to know |
|---|---|
| Personal statement | Created in MyERAS, assigned per program. See the [personal statement guide](/wiki/application/personal-statement-guide). |
| Letters of recommendation | You create entries; authors upload through the AAMC Letter Writer Portal. See [letters](/wiki/application/letters-of-recommendation). |
| USMLE transcript | You authorize release; it reaches programs through ERAS. |
| MSPE ([[mspe]]) | Handled through your school and ECFMG's supporting-documents process; check ECFMG's page for how it reaches ERAS. |
| Medical school transcript | Same route as the MSPE for IMGs. |

Document processing takes time. AAMC publishes processing times, so do not leave transcripts and the MSPE to the last week.

## What not to do

- Do not paste text with unusual formatting; write in plain text first.
- Do not leave dates approximate if you can find the exact ones.
- Do not split one activity into several experience entries to fill space. (A research project can appear once as an experience and again as scholarly work for its outputs; that is not padding.)
- Do not guess on exam-history or license questions.
