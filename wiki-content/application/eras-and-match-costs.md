ERAS and NRMP are the two big charges of application season, and both depend on your choices. ERAS charges for each program you apply to, with a lower price for the first group of programs in a specialty and a higher price after that, according to the AAMC's 2027 fee page. The NRMP charges a registration fee that covers a base number of programs, plus extra charges in some situations. This page explains how each works and how to budget. The ECFMG side (token, certification) is in [ECFMG fees](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-fees).

> [!NOTE] We do not print amounts because the AAMC and NRMP revise them each season. Use the AAMC fee page and the MyERAS fee calculator for ERAS, and the [NRMP fee page](https://www.nrmp.org/intro-to-the-match/match-fees/) for the Match.

## ERAS: what is charged

| Fee | How it works | Scales with |
|---|---|---|
| Program application fee | Charged per program. The AAMC's 2027 page describes a lower per-program price up to a stated number of programs and a higher price beyond it. Pricing is per specialty. | Number of programs and specialties |
| USMLE transcript | A transcript fee assessed once per season, according to the AAMC | Fixed per season |
| ERAS token (IMGs) | Paid to ECFMG when you request it. See the [token guide](/wiki/ecfmg/eras-token) | Once per season |
| Program signals | Available in the 2027 season for participating programs. We could not confirm a signal price. Check the AAMC page, and see [signaling explained](/wiki/application/program-signaling-explained) | Number of signals |
| Document services | Your school or other offices may charge to prepare letters, transcripts and the MSPE | Per document |

The AAMC's worked example on its fee page shows the same applicant applying to programs in two specialties and how the total is computed. It also shows a lower total for applicants approved for its Fee Assistance Program. For IMGs, check the AAMC page to see whether the program applies to you.

### Applying in more than one specialty

Pricing resets for each additional specialty, so a dual strategy can be costly. See [applying to more than one specialty](/wiki/application/applying-to-multiple-specialties) before you commit.

## NRMP: what is charged

The NRMP page describes these elements:

- **Registration** for the Main Residency Match, covering up to a stated number of program codes on your list.
- **Late registration** after the registration deadline, so register early.
- **Couples** fees for each partner if you match as a couple.
- **Extra ranks** beyond the base number, with additional charges for very long rank lists.
- **Non-refundable fees.** The NRMP states all applicant fees are non-refundable.
- **Unpaid fees.** The NRMP says applicants with outstanding fees cannot enter rank lists and can lose access to SOAP.

Details and timing are in [NRMP registration and fees](/wiki/interviews-match/nrmp-registration-and-fees).

## How to budget

1. **Fix a program count first.** Decide a range using [how many programs to apply to](/wiki/application/how-many-programs-to-apply-to), then compute ERAS costs from the AAMC calculator.
2. **Add ECFMG-side costs.** Token and any certification-related services, see [ECFMG fees](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-fees).
3. **Add NRMP costs.** Registration and, if relevant, couples and extra ranks.
4. **Add document costs.** Letters, translations, courier and MSPE issues.
5. **Add conversion costs.** Card fees and exchange rates for payment in US dollars.
6. **Keep a reserve.** A reapplication or a late change of specialty means paying a second time.

## Commonly missed costs

- Overapplying out of anxiety, since the per-program price rises beyond a threshold.
- Applying in a second specialty late in the season without budgeting for it.
- Late NRMP registration.
- Paying twice because a payment failed or a card was declined. Unpaid fees can block participation.
- Interview and relocation costs after the application, which usually dwarf the fees. See [total cost of matching](/wiki/start-here/total-cost-of-matching).

> [!IMG TIP] Opinion: apply to the number of programs your list can justify, not the number you can afford to pay for. Both matter, but spreading money across poorly matched programs rarely helps an IMG more than a tighter list does. See [building your program list](/wiki/application/building-a-program-list).

## What is different for IMGs

IMGs pay ECFMG for the token on top of the AAMC charges, may have document-translation and courier costs, and pay in US dollars from abroad. Timing is in the [ERAS timeline](/wiki/application/eras-timeline).
