The residency application runs on a fixed annual calendar. For IMGs the calendar is tighter than it looks, because several steps (ECFMG tokens, letters, exam results, visa sponsorship) depend on other people. This page gives you the order of events so you can work backward from the deadlines that matter.

> [!CHANGED] Exact dates move every year. Where this page gives timing it is typical, not official. Confirm the current dates with AAMC and NRMP: the ERAS opening date ({{fact:eras_opening_date}}) and Match Day ({{fact:match_day}}).

> [!NOTE] For the 2027 Match, NRMP says Emergency Medicine and Obstetrics and Gynecology programs use ResidencyCAS rather than ERAS, and all other specialties use ERAS. If you are applying to either of those two specialties, the ERAS steps, fees and signaling described on this page may not apply to them. Read the [Emergency Medicine](/wiki/specialties/emergency-medicine) and [OB-GYN](/wiki/specialties/obstetrics-and-gynecology) guides, and confirm the current system with NRMP.

## Who does what

| Organization | Role in your application |
|---|---|
| ECFMG / Intealth | Certifies IMGs, issues your ERAS token, transmits your certification status |
| AAMC (ERAS) | Application service where you build and submit your application |
| NRMP | Runs the Main Residency Match and your rank order list |
| Programs | Review applications, invite interviews, rank applicants |

## The season at a glance

The calendar below describes the usual shape of a season. Months are approximate.

### A year or more before: build the foundation

- Take your USMLE exams on a schedule that lets results arrive before applications are reviewed (see [when to take each Step](/wiki/exams/when-to-take-each-step)). If you may need an H-1B, plan Step 3 early (see [Step 3 and H-1B timing](/wiki/visas/step-3-and-h1b-timing)).
- Secure US clinical experience and the relationships that turn into letters. See the [[usce]] glossary entry for what counts.
- Start or continue research you can describe honestly, including your role.
- Decide on your target specialty and a realistic range of backup options.

> [!IMG TIP] Letters and clinical experience take the longest to build and are hardest to fix late. If you can only improve one thing early, start there.

### Spring and early summer: register and get your tokens

- Create or confirm your ECFMG / Intealth account and request your ERAS token when the process opens (see the [ERAS token guide](/wiki/ecfmg/eras-token)).
- Register in MyERAS with the token, and begin building your application (see the [MyERAS walkthrough](/wiki/application/myeras-walkthrough)).
- Ask your letter writers early, and give them what they need to write something specific (see [letters of recommendation](/wiki/application/letters-of-recommendation)).
- Start drafting your [personal statement](/wiki/application/personal-statement-guide) and your [experiences section](/wiki/application/eras-experiences-section).

### Summer: build and finalize

- Applicants can typically build their application before submission opens.
- Collect your transcripts, medical school documents and your Medical Student Performance Evaluation ([[mspe]]) if your school provides one.
- Build your list of programs (see [building a program list](/wiki/application/building-a-program-list) and [identifying IMG-friendly programs](/wiki/application/identifying-img-friendly-programs)). Use program websites, not just aggregator lists, to check IMG eligibility, visa sponsorship and exam requirements.
- Decide how you will use any [[program-signaling|program signals]] available to you.

### Early autumn: submit

- Submit your application when submission opens. Programs begin reviewing applications on a fixed release date, so being ready on the first day matters, but a rushed application is worse than one submitted a few days later.
- Confirm that every component has arrived: exam scores, letters, transcripts, photo if used.

### Autumn into winter: interviews

- Programs send interview invitations over several weeks. Respond quickly: slots fill.
- Prepare for virtual interviews, which are now the norm in many programs. See the [interview season overview](/wiki/interviews-match/interview-season-overview) and [virtual interview setup](/wiki/interviews-match/virtual-interview-setup).
- Keep your visa timeline in mind. Some programs only sponsor one type of visa, which can limit your options.

### Winter: rank list

- After interviews, you create your rank order list in the NRMP system (see [rank order list strategy](/wiki/interviews-match/rank-order-list-strategy)). Rank every program you would be willing to train at, in the order you prefer them.
- Never rank a program you would not attend, and do not rank based on what you think programs want to hear.

### Late winter to March: the Match

- Match Week brings the outcome (see [Match Week day by day](/wiki/interviews-match/match-week-timeline)). If you do not match, the supplemental process ([[soap]]) begins the same week, and you must be ready to act immediately. See [SOAP explained](/wiki/interviews-match/soap-explained) and [if you do not match](/wiki/interviews-match/if-you-do-not-match).
- Match Day is when you learn where you matched.

### After the Match: onboarding and visa

- Programs begin the contract and onboarding process. IMGs also begin the visa process, often with tight timelines. See [your visa timeline after the Match](/wiki/visas/visa-timeline-after-the-match), the [J-1 process](/wiki/visas/j1-visa-process) and the [H-1B guide](/wiki/visas/h1b-for-residents).

## Planning backward: a checklist

1. List the date your exams, letters and experience must be complete.
2. Subtract the time each step really takes (not the time you hope it takes).
3. Mark the dates that cannot move: token requests, submission, interview invitations, rank list deadline.
4. Review the plan every month and adjust early rather than late.

## Common timeline mistakes

- Waiting for an exam result before starting everything else.
- Asking for letters only weeks before the application is due.
- Applying without checking each program's IMG, visa and exam rules.
- Assuming the dates from last year still apply.
- Having no plan for what to do the week the Match results come out.
