The **NRMP** (National Resident Matching Program) operates the Main Residency Match. After interviews, applicants submit a [[rank-order-list]] of programs and programs rank applicants. A matching algorithm then pairs them, and results are released on Match Day.

The NRMP also publishes data on match rates, including for IMGs, which is a useful primary source when you assess how competitive a specialty is. It is separate from [[eras]], which handles the application itself.
