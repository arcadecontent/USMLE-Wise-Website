The **NBME** (National Board of Medical Examiners) is an independent nonprofit organization that, together with the Federation of State Medical Boards (FSMB), sponsors the [[usmle|USMLE]]. It develops the exam content and also offers medical education assessment products.

Candidates most often meet the NBME through its **self-assessments**, practice forms that estimate readiness for a Step exam. They are widely used in preparation, though they are practice tools, not official predictions.

Note that [[img|IMGs]] do not apply for the USMLE through NBME directly: certification-related applications go through [[ecfmg|ECFMG]] / [[intealth|Intealth]], and since January 2026 FSMB handles Step exam registration and services for IMGs. Check the official pages for the current arrangement. See the [self-assessments guide](/wiki/exams/nbme-self-assessments).
