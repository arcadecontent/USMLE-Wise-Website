A **categorical** position is a residency spot that includes the whole training program in a specialty, from [[pgy|PGY-1]] to the final year, for example three years in internal medicine. If you match into a categorical position, you are expected to continue through the program, subject to meeting its requirements.

It differs from a [[preliminary-year|preliminary]] position, which covers only one year and does not guarantee a place afterwards. Some specialties, such as radiology or anesthesiology, may ask applicants to secure a separate first-year position, so the structure varies by field and program.

Programs list the types of positions they offer in [[eras|ERAS]] and in the [[nrmp|NRMP]] Match. Read each program's description carefully.
