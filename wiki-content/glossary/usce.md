**USCE** (US clinical experience) means clinical time spent in a US healthcare setting, such as observerships, externships or hands-on rotations. Many residency programs expect or prefer IMGs to have some, and it is the main way IMGs earn US letters of recommendation.

What counts differs by program. Hands-on experience is generally valued more than purely observational experience, and recent experience is generally valued more than old experience. See [[observership]] for the most common type, and check each program's stated requirements.
