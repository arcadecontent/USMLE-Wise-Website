A **preliminary year** (or prelim) is a one-year [[pgy|PGY-1]] position, most often in internal medicine or surgery. It gives a year of broad clinical training but does not by itself lead to board eligibility in a specialty, and it does not guarantee a spot afterwards.

Applicants use prelim positions in two main ways. Some specialties that start at PGY-2 require a separate first year, so applicants rank a preliminary program alongside advanced ones. Others take a prelim spot hoping to move into a [[categorical|categorical]] position later, which is a less certain path.

A transitional year is a related one-year option with rotations across several fields. Rules and outcomes differ by program, so check each one. Positions are matched through the [[nrmp|NRMP]] and applied for in [[eras|ERAS]].
