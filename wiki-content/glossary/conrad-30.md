**Conrad 30** is a program under section 214(l) of the Immigration and Nationality Act. A state health department can recommend up to 30 foreign physicians a year for a waiver of the J-1 two-year home-country requirement. In exchange, the physician works full time for at least three years, in H-1B status, at a facility in or serving a federally designated shortage area.

Each state sets its own application rules, deadlines and priorities, and the State Department reviews requests. The program depends on a sunset date that Congress has repeatedly extended, so its current status is something to check before planning around it.

See [the Conrad 30 guide](/wiki/visas/conrad-30-waiver), [J-1 waiver options](/wiki/visas/j1-waiver-options) and [the two-year home residency requirement](/wiki/visas/two-year-home-residency-rule). The [[ds-2019]] and visa show whether you are subject to the requirement.
