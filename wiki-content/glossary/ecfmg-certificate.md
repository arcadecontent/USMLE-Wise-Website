The **ECFMG Certificate** is the document [[ecfmg|ECFMG]] issues to an [[img|IMG]] who has met all its requirements: an eligible medical school, verified credentials, passing [[usmle|USMLE]] Step 1 and Step 2 CK, and the clinical and communication skills requirement through a Pathway.

It is required to enter [[acgme|ACGME]]-accredited residency in the US. It is not a license to practice; state medical boards issue licenses, and Step 3 is usually required for that. If the certificate was earned through a Pathway, it can be subject to expiration unless it qualifies to be made valid indefinitely.

See the [ECFMG certification overview](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-certification-overview) for the full list of requirements.
