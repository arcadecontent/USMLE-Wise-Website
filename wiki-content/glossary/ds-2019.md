The **DS-2019** is the Certificate of Eligibility for Exchange Visitor (J-1) Status. It is issued by the exchange program's sponsor after it registers you in [[sevis]], and it is the form you present at the visa interview and at the US border. For most IMG residents the sponsor is [[ecfmg]], not the residency program.

The form lists your SEVIS ID, your program dates and the sponsor. Those dates matter: they set how long you may stay for training, and they must match what you tell the consular officer. A new DS-2019 is needed when training is extended or renewed, and the form should be kept safe and signed as the sponsor instructs.

The DS-2019 is not a visa. It lets you apply for one. See [the J-1 visa process](/wiki/visas/j1-visa-process), and [preparing for your visa interview](/wiki/visas/visa-interview-preparation). Your DS-2019 and visa also show whether you are subject to the [two-year home residency requirement](/wiki/visas/two-year-home-residency-rule).
