**PGY** stands for postgraduate year. It counts the years of graduate medical education since medical school. **PGY-1** is the first year of residency (often called the intern year), **PGY-2** is the second, and so on. Programs are described by their length, so a three-year internal medicine residency runs from PGY-1 to PGY-3.

PGY level reflects training stage, not age or time since graduation, and it often determines supervision, responsibility and pay scale at a hospital. Applicants to a first-year position are often described as applying for PGY-1.

Some specialties begin with a [[preliminary-year|preliminary year]] and others include the first year within the program, so the position type ([[categorical|categorical]] or preliminary) affects what happens after PGY-1. Residency positions are filled through the [[nrmp|NRMP]] Match.
