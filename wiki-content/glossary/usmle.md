The **USMLE** (United States Medical Licensing Examination) is the exam series used for medical licensure in the US. It has three Steps: Step 1, Step 2 CK (Clinical Knowledge) and Step 3. It is sponsored by the Federation of State Medical Boards (FSMB) and the [[nbme|National Board of Medical Examiners]].

For [[img|IMGs]], passing Step 1 and Step 2 CK is part of the requirements for [[ecfmg|ECFMG]] Certification. Step 3 is usually taken later and is required for licensure in most states. Step 1 is reported pass/fail only.

Registration and policies change, including who handles IMG registration, so use the official USMLE and ECFMG pages for current details. See the [Step 1 guide](/wiki/exams/step-1) and [USMLE eligibility for IMGs](/wiki/exams/usmle-eligibility-for-imgs).
