To train in the US as an international medical graduate ([[img]]), you must pass the required USMLE exams, earn [[ecfmg-certificate|ECFMG certification]], submit a residency application through [[eras]], interview, and match through the [[nrmp]]. This page shows those steps in the order most applicants meet them and points to the detailed guide for each one.

The years below are labelled relative to the year you apply (Year 0 is the application year), because your calendar will differ from anyone else's.

## The big picture

| Stage | What happens | Who controls it |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility | Confirm your school and graduation year qualify | ECFMG / Intealth |
| Exams | Step 1, Step 2 CK, later Step 3 | USMLE (NBME and FSMB) |
| Certification | Credential verification, exams, English, certificate | ECFMG |
| Experience | US clinical experience, research, letters | You, plus hosts and mentors |
| Application | ERAS, personal statement, signals, program list | AAMC |
| Interviews and Match | Interviews, rank list, Match Day | Programs and NRMP |
| Visa | J-1 or H-1B (or other status) | State Department, USCIS, ECFMG |

## Year -3 to -2: Foundations

This is the stage to check eligibility and decide whether the path is realistic for you.

1. **Verify your school and graduation year.** Start with the [World Directory and school eligibility](/wiki/ecfmg/world-directory-and-school-eligibility) and the [accreditation requirement](/wiki/ecfmg/accreditation-requirement).
2. **Create your MyIntealth account** and begin credential verification. See [setting up MyIntealth](/wiki/ecfmg/myintealth-account) and [EPIC credential verification](/wiki/ecfmg/epic-credential-verification). Verification can be slow because it depends on your school and licensing body.
3. **Understand the full path** with the [ECFMG certification overview](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-certification-overview).
4. **Build a rough budget** using the [total cost guide](/wiki/start-here/total-cost-of-matching).
5. **Start thinking about specialty.** Read [choosing a specialty](/wiki/specialties/choosing-a-specialty) before you commit years to one direction.

> [!IMG TIP] Credential verification and document problems are common sources of delay. Start them early, even if your exam plans are not final.

## Year -2 to -1: Exams

The two exams every IMG needs for ECFMG certification are Step 1 and Step 2 CK.

- Read the [Step 1 guide](/wiki/exams/step-1) and the [Step 2 CK guide](/wiki/exams/step-2-ck).
- Decide the order and spacing with [when to take each Step](/wiki/exams/when-to-take-each-step).
- Check [USMLE eligibility for IMGs](/wiki/exams/usmle-eligibility-for-imgs) and [how scheduling works](/wiki/exams/scheduling-with-prometric).
- Use [NBME self-assessments](/wiki/exams/nbme-self-assessments) to decide when you are ready, and know the [attempt limits](/wiki/exams/usmle-attempt-limits-and-retakes) before you register.

Step 1 is reported pass/fail, so Step 2 CK is where a three-digit score can still help you. See [Step 2 CK scores and what programs look for](/wiki/exams/step-2-ck-score-and-what-it-means).

## Year -1 to 0: Experience, research and the application

Programs do not only read scores. They look for evidence that you can function in a US clinical team.

- **US clinical experience:** [USCE explained](/wiki/clinical-experience/usce-explained), [how to get it](/wiki/clinical-experience/how-to-get-usce) and [how much programs want](/wiki/clinical-experience/how-much-usce-programs-want).
- **Letters:** [turning USCE into a strong letter](/wiki/clinical-experience/turning-usce-into-a-letter) and [letters of recommendation](/wiki/application/letters-of-recommendation).
- **Research:** [why research matters for IMGs](/wiki/research/why-research-matters-for-imgs), and when it does not.
- **Application materials:** [ERAS timeline](/wiki/application/eras-timeline), [MyERAS walkthrough](/wiki/application/myeras-walkthrough), [experiences section](/wiki/application/eras-experiences-section) and [personal statement](/wiki/application/personal-statement-guide).
- **ERAS token:** IMGs need a token to start their application. See [the ERAS token](/wiki/ecfmg/eras-token).

> [!WARNING] Several steps depend on documents and approvals from third parties (schools, hospitals, letter writers, ECFMG). Add buffer time. A late letter or verification can cost you an entire application season.

## Year 0 to +1: Match and the move

- Build your [rank order list](/wiki/interviews-match/rank-order-list-strategy), learn [how the algorithm works](/wiki/interviews-match/how-the-match-algorithm-works) and what [Match week](/wiki/interviews-match/match-week-timeline) looks like.
- Know the fallback: [SOAP](/wiki/interviews-match/soap-explained) and [what to do if you do not match](/wiki/interviews-match/if-you-do-not-match).
- Start the visa process right after the Match: [your visa timeline after the Match](/wiki/visas/visa-timeline-after-the-match). Compare options in [J-1 vs H-1B](/wiki/visas/j1-vs-h1b).
- If you may want an H-1B, read [Step 3 and H-1B timing](/wiki/visas/step-3-and-h1b-timing) early, because Step 3 is often planned around that decision. The [Step 3 guide](/wiki/exams/step-3) covers the exam itself.

## What can change the plan

| Situation | Typical effect |
|---|---|
| Failed exam attempt | Delay and a weaker application; see [what to do after failing](/wiki/exams/what-to-do-after-failing-a-step) |
| Long gap since graduation | Programs may look for recent US experience |
| Not matching | A reapplication year; see [reapplicant strategy](/wiki/application/reapplicant-strategy) |
| Visa limits | Some programs sponsor only one visa type |

For a realistic time estimate, read [how long it takes](/wiki/start-here/how-long-does-it-take).

## Rules, practice and opinion

The exams, ECFMG certification and the Match are official requirements. Typical timing, how much experience to gather and how many programs to target are common practice, not rules. Our view (opinion): start earlier than feels necessary, keep a single tracker of documents and deadlines, and revisit your plan after each exam result rather than committing to a fixed calendar.

> [!NOTE] Policies and portals change. In January 2026, registration and administration of USMLE services for IMGs moved to FSMB, while ECFMG continues to determine eligibility for ECFMG certification. Always confirm current steps on the official sites before you act.
