Match Week is five days in which you learn whether you matched, and unmatched applicants use SOAP to pursue open positions. The structure is stable year to year, but NRMP sets exact dates and times annually. This page describes what happens each day. To see the dates for your cycle, use the Match Week and SOAP calendar linked from NRMP's [Match Week](https://www.nrmp.org/residency-applicants/match-week/) and [SOAP](https://www.nrmp.org/residency-applicants/soap/) pages.

## Before Match Week

Several things must be settled before Monday, not during the week.

- **Your rank order list is certified.** After the certification deadline you cannot change it. See [building your rank order list](/wiki/interviews-match/rank-order-list-strategy).
- **Your NRMP verification is complete.** NRMP requires IMGs to be verified by ECFMG as having met the examination requirements for certification by the rank order list certification deadline. Unverified IMGs are withdrawn from the Match and cannot take part in SOAP. Verification status shows in NRMP's R3 system.
- **Your SOAP materials are ready,** in case you need them: ERAS application, letters, transcript, personal statement and a plan for your limits. See [SOAP explained](/wiki/interviews-match/soap-explained).
- **Your contact details are current** in R3 and ERAS, since programs and NRMP will email you.

## Day by day

| Day | What happens | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | In the morning (Eastern time), applicants are told whether they matched, but not where. Notification is by email and visible in R3. SOAP begins for eligible applicants. | Check R3. If unmatched or partially matched and eligible, view the List of Unfilled Programs. |
| Tuesday | Programs review SOAP applications in their application service, interview, and begin creating preference lists in R3. | Apply to unfilled programs, respond to contacts, prepare for fast interviews. |
| Wednesday | SOAP continues. | Keep responding promptly. |
| Thursday | Offers go out in the final rounds. NRMP describes a series of four SOAP offer rounds across the SOAP period. | Respond to offers within each round's window. |
| Friday | At midday Eastern, applicants and programs receive match results by email and in R3. | Confirm your program and note instructions from NRMP and the program. |

> [!NOTE] The exact timing and number of SOAP rounds is on NRMP's official calendar. The table above summarizes NRMP's published descriptions and does not replace it.

### Monday: matched or not

At the start of Match Week you get one piece of information: matched or not matched. If matched, you wait for Friday to learn where. If unmatched or partially matched, SOAP opens for you. NRMP also warns that applicants who are not eligible for SOAP cannot contact Match-participating programs until SOAP concludes.

### Tuesday to Thursday: SOAP

Programs with unfilled positions are listed in R3. Eligible applicants apply through the application service the program designates, programs contact those they want, and positions are offered to applicants in rounds based on programs' preference lists. Accepted offers are binding. Details are in [SOAP explained](/wiki/interviews-match/soap-explained).

### Friday: Match Day

Results are released at midday Eastern time per NRMP's materials. Your match is a binding commitment under the Match Participation Agreement, enforced by NRMP. Read the glossary entry for [[match-day]].

## What 2026 looked like

NRMP's 2026 report gives context for the scale of Match Week: 48,050 active applicants competed for 41,126 PGY-1 positions in the Match. The algorithm left 2,862 positions unfilled, and with 109 positions in programs that did not submit a rank list, 2,971 were unfilled; 2,851 were placed in SOAP. The overall fill rate after SOAP was 99.3 percent.

## After Friday: what an IMG needs to do next

- **Contact the program** to learn onboarding steps: contract, documents, start date and health requirements.
- **ECFMG certification.** You must hold ECFMG certification before starting training. If any requirement is still pending, tell the program and ECFMG ([ECFMG certification](https://www.ecfmg.org/certification/)).
- **Visa.** Most IMGs need a visa. The program tells you whether it sponsors J-1, H-1B, or neither, and the steps start immediately. See [your visa timeline after the Match](/wiki/visas/visa-timeline-after-the-match) and [J-1 vs H-1B](/wiki/visas/j1-vs-h1b).
- **Expect deadlines.** Programs have strict dates for paperwork, and delays can put your start date at risk.

> [!WARNING] A match is a binding commitment under the Match Participation Agreement, and NRMP investigates suspected violations. Read the agreement before you communicate with other programs after results.
