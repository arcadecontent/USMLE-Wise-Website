The USMLE program has changed several rules in the last few years, and old videos and forum threads often describe a system that no longer exists. Below is a dated log of changes we could verify on official USMLE pages, who each one affects, and where to read the notice.

> [!NOTE] This is a log of what we verified, not a complete history. When this page and an official notice disagree, the official notice wins.

## Log of verified changes

## What each change means in practice

### Step 2 CS discontinuation (2021)

Clinical skills are no longer assessed by a USMLE exam. For IMGs, ECFMG now uses Pathways to meet its clinical and communication skills requirements, and details are in the [ECFMG certification overview](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-certification-overview). Older Step 2 CS results still appear on transcripts, with an explanatory note.

### Four attempts per Step (2021)

The current limit is {{fact:usmle_attempts_per_step}} attempts per Step. If you have already made several attempts, read [attempt limits and retakes](/wiki/exams/usmle-attempt-limits-and-retakes) carefully before registering.

### Pass/fail Step 1 (2022)

The change moved competitive weight to Step 2 CK, where a numeric score is still reported. See the [Step 1 guide](/wiki/exams/step-1) and the [Step 2 CK score guide](/wiki/exams/step-2-ck-score-and-what-it-means).

### Step 2 CK passing standard (2025)

The standard is reviewed periodically. Confirm the figure that applies to your test date on the USMLE scoring page, because the standard that applies depends on when you test.

### FSMB registration for IMGs (2026)

> [!CHANGED] Since January 2026, IMGs apply for USMLE Steps through FSMB, not through ECFMG's MyIntealth. Applications not processed before the transition had to be resubmitted to the new provider, according to USMLE.

If you are following an older walkthrough, switch to the [IMG eligibility guide](/wiki/exams/usmle-eligibility-for-imgs) and the USMLE notice. Name and document details moved too, so see [name and document issues](/wiki/ecfmg/name-and-document-issues).

### New exam format (May 2026)

> [!CHANGED] In May 2026, Step 2 CK and Step 1 began using shorter 30-minute blocks. USMLE says exam content is unchanged, and examinees with approved extra time or break time receive an updated decision letter.

Practice with the current tutorial and interactive test. Pacing drills built around the old 60-minute blocks no longer match the real exam.

## How to monitor changes

1. **Read the Bulletin for your test year.** The USMLE Bulletin of Information is the rule book, updated annually.
2. **Check usmle.org news** before you register and again a few weeks before your exam.
3. **Check ECFMG and FSMB pages** for certification and registration rules, since each handles a different part of the process.
4. **Note effective dates.** Many changes depend on the date of application or exam, not on the announcement date.
5. **Screenshot or save the notice** you relied on when you made a plan, so you can compare if something changes.
6. **Treat prep company posts as secondhand.** They are useful for alerts but verify them on the official page.

Rules also change in ways this log may miss. If you spot something not listed here, check usmle.org before acting on it.
