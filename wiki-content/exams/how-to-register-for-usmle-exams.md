To register for USMLE Step 1, Step 2 CK or Step 3 as an IMG, you apply through FSMB's USMLE portal, not through ECFMG, once your MyIntealth account is verified and your Application for ECFMG Certification has been accepted. After you pay, you choose an eligibility period, receive a scheduling permit by email, and book your seat with Prometric.

This page marks where each statement comes from. **Rule (Bulletin)** means the USMLE Bulletin of Information. **Rule (usmle.org)** means another official USMLE page. **Practice** means what usually works, and **opinion** is ours. We could not read FSMB's or ECFMG's own websites while researching this page, so anything that lives only there is hedged and you should confirm it in your FSMB account.

> [!CHANGED] Since January 12, 2026, IMGs register for USMLE through FSMB, not through ECFMG's MyIntealth. Guides, videos and forum posts that tell you to apply for Step 1 or Step 2 CK inside MyIntealth describe the old process. MyIntealth still matters, because it is where your identity is verified and your ECFMG application is tracked.

## How to register for USMLE Step 1 as an IMG: the short version

The same sequence applies to Step 2 CK. Step 3 has extra requirements, covered further down.

1. **Verify your identity in MyIntealth.** Create your account and complete the notarized identity form. See [setting up MyIntealth](/wiki/ecfmg/myintealth-account).
2. **Check that your school and graduation year are eligible.** See [USMLE eligibility for IMGs](/wiki/exams/usmle-eligibility-for-imgs).
3. **Submit your Application for ECFMG Certification and wait for it to be accepted.** USMLE's transition FAQ says the application must be accepted before you can apply for your first Step exam (Rule, usmle.org).
4. **Create your FSMB USMLE portal account.** Your name of record must exactly match your name of record with Intealth.
5. **Apply for the Step in the FSMB portal.** You select an eligibility period, pay the fees and submit.
6. **Wait for approval and your scheduling permit.** The permit arrives by email.
7. **Book your seat with Prometric** inside your eligibility period. See [scheduling with Prometric](/wiki/exams/scheduling-with-prometric).
8. **Take the exam** with identification that matches the name on your permit. See the [exam day checklist](/wiki/exams/exam-day-rules-and-checklist).

You register for each Step separately. If you are deciding which Step to book first, see [when to take each Step](/wiki/exams/when-to-take-each-step).

## Who registers you now: FSMB, not ECFMG

Rule (Bulletin): "All students and graduates of international medical schools should log in to FSMB's USMLE portal to register for the Step exams." This applies to Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3.

The two organizations now do different jobs:

| Organization | Its job in your registration |
|---|---|
| FSMB | Runs the USMLE portal where IMGs apply, pay, choose an eligibility period and receive the scheduling permit |
| ECFMG / [[intealth|Intealth]] | Decides whether you are eligible for ECFMG Certification, and holds your MyIntealth account and certification application |
| Prometric | Books your seat and handles appointment changes |

Rule (usmle.org): FSMB confirms your eligibility with ECFMG when you register for each Step, and Step 1 and Step 2 CK remain ECFMG certification requirements. See the [ECFMG certification overview](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-certification-overview) for the full list.

US students and graduates register through NBME, not FSMB. If a guide tells you to use MyUSMLE, it is written for US graduates, so do not follow it as an IMG.

## What you need before you can register

| What you need | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A MyIntealth account with verified identity | You need your MyIntealth ID. See the [MyIntealth guide](/wiki/ecfmg/myintealth-account) | Rule (usmle.org) |
| An accepted Application for ECFMG Certification | USMLE says it must be accepted before you apply for your first Step | Rule (usmle.org) |
| An FSMB USMLE portal account | Create it from the links on usmle.org. USMLE's FAQ refers to an FSMB account-creation charge, but we could not confirm who pays it or how it is credited, so check FSMB's current fee page before paying | Rule (usmle.org); the charge details are unconfirmed |
| A name that matches exactly | FSMB requires your name of record to exactly match your name of record with Intealth. See [name and document issues](/wiki/ecfmg/name-and-document-issues) | Rule (usmle.org) |
| An eligible school and status | A student or graduate of a school listed in the World Directory as meeting ECFMG eligibility requirements, both when you apply and on exam day | Rule (Bulletin) |

Practice: fix any name mismatch before you apply. It is the most common cause of avoidable delay, and it also matters on exam day when the test center compares your ID with your permit.

When you register for your first Step, FSMB issues your USMLE ID. IDs issued earlier by ECFMG remain valid. See [the MyIntealth guide](/wiki/ecfmg/myintealth-account) for how the two IDs differ.

## Eligibility period and scheduling permit

The eligibility period is the window in which you must test. The permit is the email document that lets you book.

- **Eligibility period.** Rule (usmle.org): you choose it when you apply, and you must test within the {{fact:usmle_eligibility_period}} eligibility period assigned at application. The Bulletin says you select an eligibility period but does not give the length. We could not see which window dates FSMB offers IMGs, so check your portal.
- **Scheduling permit.** Rule (Bulletin): after registration is complete, a scheduling permit is emailed to you, and you then schedule with Prometric. USMLE does not let you test without a permit.
- **How long it takes.** Rule (usmle.org): allow {{fact:usmle_permit_wait}} for approval and for the permit to arrive.
- **How early you can book.** Rule (Bulletin): scheduling may not be available more than {{fact:usmle_scheduling_advance}} in advance, and test dates are first-come, first-served. Rule (usmle.org): permits are not sent more than {{fact:usmle_scheduling_advance}} before a selected eligibility period begins.

Where to find the permit: it is emailed, so search your inbox and spam for the address you used on the application, then check your FSMB account. We could not read FSMB's portal, so we cannot say which screen shows it.

Practice (opinion): pick a window that puts your target date in the middle, not at the end. That leaves room for a reschedule, a visa delay or an illness. See [how to reschedule a USMLE exam](/wiki/exams/how-to-reschedule-a-usmle-exam).

Rule (usmle.org): these figures come from USMLE's apply-for-exams page. The page describes the region fee as not being a USMLE fee, and notes a local tax for exams taken in India. Fee pages are updated, so treat the table as a checked snapshot and confirm the total in your FSMB checkout.

Points to know:

- **Step 3 fee.** USMLE's own pages showed two different Step 3 amounts when we checked in November 2026, so we do not quote one. Use the figure FSMB shows when you apply.
- **Older figures.** Some guides still quote higher figures and an international surcharge from the ECFMG-run process. We could not read FSMB's or ECFMG's fee pages, so we have not reconciled the two schemes. Pay only the total your FSMB checkout shows.
- **No refunds.** Rule (Bulletin): fees are non-refundable and non-transferable.
- **Other charges.** Rescheduling, extending and some FSMB account charges are separate from the exam fee. See [how to reschedule a USMLE exam](/wiki/exams/how-to-reschedule-a-usmle-exam).
- **Budgeting.** See [total cost of matching](/wiki/start-here/total-cost-of-matching) and [ECFMG and Intealth fees](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-fees) for the other lines.

> [!WARNING] Opinion: pay fees only inside the official FSMB portal, and be wary of any third party that offers to register you for a fee. Reach the portal from the links on usmle.org.

## If you need more time: the extension

Rule (usmle.org): you may request a one-time, contiguous eligibility period extension. You can request it only after your permit is issued, and the request must reach your registering organization no later than {{fact:usmle_extension_request_days}} after the end of your original eligibility period. A fee applies, and we could not confirm the amount FSMB charges IMGs. If you miss both the original period and the extension, a new application and fee are required.

Practice: treat the extension as a safety net, not a plan. For when to reschedule, extend or reapply, see the decision table in [how to reschedule a USMLE exam](/wiki/exams/how-to-reschedule-a-usmle-exam).

## Booking with Prometric

Once you have your permit, you schedule on Prometric's website with the details on the permit. Availability varies by country, center and month, and USMLE cannot guarantee a seat. See [scheduling with Prometric](/wiki/exams/scheduling-with-prometric) for the booking process and [test centers outside the US](/wiki/exams/international-test-centers) for how to compare centers.

Practice (opinion): apply early enough in your planning that the window you want is still open, and compare several centers and weeks of dates before you commit to travel.

## Where Step 3 is different

| Point | Step 1 and Step 2 CK | Step 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Who registers IMGs | FSMB | FSMB |
| Prerequisites | Eligible school, plus the steps above | Passed Step 1 and Step 2 CK, and a valid, unexpired ECFMG Certificate (Rule, Bulletin) |
| Where it is given | Prometric centers in the US and many other countries | Only in the US and its territories (Rule, Bulletin) |
| Region fee | Listed for exams outside the US and Canada | None listed |
| Fee | {{fact:step1_fee}} and {{fact:step2ck_fee}} | {{fact:step3_fee}} |
| Training year | Not applicable | One postgraduate year in an accredited US program is recommended, not required (Rule, Bulletin) |

Because Step 3 is given only in the US, plan travel and any visa well before your window. See [Step 3](/wiki/exams/step-3) and [B-1/B-2 for interviews and exams](/wiki/visas/b1-b2-for-interviews-and-exams). Many IMGs plan Step 3 around residency and work visa timing; see [when to take each Step](/wiki/exams/when-to-take-each-step).

## Reporting preference

Rule (Bulletin): IMGs must specify their score reporting preference on the application with FSMB. Read the Bulletin's section on score reporting before you submit, and make the choice deliberately.

## Common mistakes

- **Following a pre-2026 guide** that tells you to register in MyIntealth.
- **Name mismatch** between your passport, your Intealth record and your FSMB account.
- **Applying before your ECFMG application is accepted.**
- **Choosing the shortest window** you think you need, then being unable to reschedule.
- **Booking travel before the Prometric appointment is confirmed.**
- **Assuming a figure from a forum or old guide is current.** Fees and rules change.

> [!NOTE] USMLE has announced plans to reduce the number of Step administrations each year beginning in January 2029 (designated testing dates), and says eligibility periods and scheduling arrangements may change. This does not affect registering today, but check the [USMLE designated testing dates page](https://www.usmle.org/designated-testing-dates) if you are planning far ahead. This page summarizes official sources as reviewed in November 2026, and the current Bulletin is the final authority.

## What to do next

Confirm your eligibility, then start your registration steps in order. Once you hold a permit, book Prometric early in your window. If your plans change, read [how to reschedule a USMLE exam](/wiki/exams/how-to-reschedule-a-usmle-exam), and for the limits on repeat attempts see [attempt limits and retakes](/wiki/exams/usmle-attempt-limits-and-retakes).
