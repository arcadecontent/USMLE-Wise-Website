If you are an IMG, you can register for USMLE Step 1 or Step 2 CK when you are officially enrolled in, or a graduate of, a non-US medical school that is listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools as meeting ECFMG eligibility requirements, and you meet the other criteria in the Bulletin. You must meet those criteria both **when you apply and on the day of your exam**.

## Eligibility by Step

| Step | Key requirements for non-US graduates |
|---|---|
| Step 1 | Enrolled or graduate of an eligible school, plus other Bulletin criteria |
| Step 2 CK | Same as Step 1 |
| Step 3 | Passed Step 1 and Step 2 CK; MD-equivalent from a listed school; valid, unexpired ECFMG Certificate; other Bulletin criteria |

USMLE recommends, but does not require, that Step 3 candidates have completed or be near completion of at least one postgraduate training year in an accredited US program.

## Two organizations, two jobs

Two separate bodies are involved, and mixing them up is a common source of confusion.

- **ECFMG / Intealth** decides whether you meet the requirements for ECFMG certification, including medical school eligibility. See [ECFMG certification overview](/wiki/ecfmg/ecfmg-certification-overview) and [World Directory and school eligibility](/wiki/ecfmg/world-directory-and-school-eligibility).
- **FSMB** manages USMLE exam services for IMGs, including registration, since January 12, 2026. Before that, ECFMG handled Step 1 and Step 2 CK services for IMGs.

The Bulletin states that IMGs should apply for Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3 through the FSMB website. ECFMG still determines IMG eligibility for ECFMG certification, and Step 1 and Step 2 CK remain required for the medical science requirement.

> [!CHANGED] In January 2026, USMLE services for IMGs moved from ECFMG's MyIntealth portal to FSMB's USMLE portal. Guides written before then may describe the old process. Check the current instructions before you register.

## Medical school and graduation requirements

Your school must be listed in the World Directory with the right eligibility information, and there is also an accreditation requirement for graduates of non-US schools. Read [the accreditation requirement](/wiki/ecfmg/accreditation-requirement) because it affects which graduation years and schools qualify. Do not assume your school is eligible because graduates have registered in the past.

## Rules that can make you ineligible

1. **Dismissal or withdrawal from medical school.** This makes you ineligible even if you are appealing.
2. **Leave of absence.** Check with your registering organization before applying or testing.
3. **Four or more attempts at a Step without passing.** If you have made four or more attempts at any Step (including incomplete attempts) without passing, you cannot apply for any Step. Attempts at the former Step 2 Clinical Skills count. See [attempt limits and retakes](/wiki/exams/usmle-attempt-limits-and-retakes).
4. **Prior US license.** If you already hold a US physician license based on other exams, you may not be eligible.
5. **Passed Steps.** You cannot retake a Step you passed, with narrow exceptions.

> [!WARNING] You must report changes in eligibility to your registering organization before your test date. Read the Bulletin's cautions on what follows if you test when not eligible.

## Time limits and state boards

The Bulletin does not impose a fixed years-since-graduation limit for registering, but many state medical boards require all Steps to be completed within a set period for licensure. Check the board in the state where you hope to train and practice. Programs may also have their own preferences about graduation year, covered in [how programs screen applications](/wiki/application/program-screening-filters).

## Step-by-step: confirming eligibility

1. Search for your school in the World Directory and read the eligibility details.
2. Create or log in to your MyIntealth account and confirm your ECFMG requirements. See [setting up MyIntealth](/wiki/ecfmg/myintealth-account).
3. Read the current Bulletin.
4. Register through the route the Bulletin gives for IMGs.
5. Record your eligibility period and plan your seat. See [scheduling with Prometric](/wiki/exams/scheduling-with-prometric).

## IMG-specific considerations

- Check the Bulletin's name and identification requirements before you register, and fix any mismatch between your records and your ID early.
- You need a valid ECFMG Certificate to be eligible for Step 3 as a non-US graduate.
- Your timeline for ECFMG certification and your timeline for the exams interact; see [when to take each Step](/wiki/exams/when-to-take-each-step).

> [!NOTE] Eligibility is determined at the time of application and again on exam day. If anything changes, contact your registering organization first. Official rules are in the Bulletin; this page is a summary.
